Ten more techniques, same horse-person, same floor

Round 8 adds ten further rendering techniques (numbers 11–20 of the exploration) for the same anthropomorphic horse character and seamless isometric grass tile, so all twenty can be compared side by side with round 7. The engine-view panel shows the floor exactly as the game draws it: the square texture squashed 2:1 into stitched diamonds (drawTexturedPaintTile). The seam strip repeats the raw tile flat; the score is the mean edge mismatch (<25 reads stitch-free — the currently shipped grass-tile scores 66). Anime Cel

A Saturday-morning cel, one frame at a time. Clean two-tone cel shading, thin selective outlines, glossy accent highlights.

Technique rules

  • Outline: thin, selective, colored darker than the fill.
  • Shading: exactly two tones per material plus one gloss highlight.
  • Color: clear and bright, free hue.
  • Avoid: painterly noise, dithering, heavy black outlines.
