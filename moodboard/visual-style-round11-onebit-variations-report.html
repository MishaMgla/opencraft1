One bit, ten voices

Ten variations of the One-Bit Ink finalist — every one strictly two colors, differing only in mark-making: dots, carved strokes, screentone, dither, brush, flat shapes, spot blacks, hatching, and pure silhouette (plus one inverted white-on-black world). Each carries the full cast — anthro horse, pigeon man, Jesus, pinniped man — on its own seamless ground in the engine's diamond projection. All prompts are saved in web/assets/manifest.json; the per-style phrasing is the reusable style suffix.
