opencraft1 · visual identity exploration · round 9 · cast test · 2026-07-07

Six finalists, four citizens each

The six shortlisted techniques now carry a full cast — anthro horse, pigeon man, Jesus, and pinniped man — standing together on their own grass in the engine's diamond projection. This is the deciding test: a style is only an identity if every new character automatically belongs to the same world. All prompts are saved in web/assets/manifest.json; the per-style phrasing is the reusable style suffix.

FINALIST 1 / 6

Watercolor Wash

The gentlest world of the six. The cast stays coherent — soft washes unify horse, bird, robe, and seal — but light characters can sink into the pale meadow; darker wash accents (like the pigeon's vest) fix it.
